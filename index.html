UNIT-HOLDER FAQ

Common questions

Does a Unit in my brokerage account mean I hold a freely tradable DOG token?

No. The Unit is a restricted book-entry instrument. The presence of a public market for DOG does not make the Unit freely transferable or tradable.

Can I sell my Unit on PancakeSwap?

No. PancakeSwap trades blockchain tokens held in compatible digital wallets; it does not trade the restricted book-entry Unit displayed by a broker or transfer agent.

Does the market price shown here determine the value of my Unit?

No. The displayed price reflects activity in a particular decentralized-exchange pool. It may involve very low liquidity and does not establish the value, transferability, or redeemability of a restricted Unit.

Is DogecoinCash.com the developers' official token website?

No. This website is an informational resource for Unit holders. It does not claim to be operated by the DOG token's developers or to replace any developer website.

How can I avoid look-alike tokens?

Always compare the entire contract address—not merely the name or symbol. The contract referenced here is 0x9596a56c73CA6f50CBd05cB8D85865F75659dC78 on BNB Smart Chain.
